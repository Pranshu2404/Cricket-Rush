How to play

One button: tap anywhere, or press SPACE.

Batting: tap as the ball reaches the batter. The batter picks the shot from the line and length. Balls outside off go to the off side, balls on the pads go to the leg side, short balls get pulled or cut. PERFECT timing goes up and over the rope. Early or late tapping costs power, and it can bring an edge.

Running: after the shot, tap once for each run. The run box in the corner turns amber or red when a run-out is likely.

Bowlers: each over is a new bowler. That can be right-arm or left-arm, fast, medium, off-spin, leg-spin or left-arm spin. Pace comes at you quickly, and spin turns off the pitch.

Leaving: if you don't tap, the batter leaves the ball. If it's on the stumps, you're bowled.
